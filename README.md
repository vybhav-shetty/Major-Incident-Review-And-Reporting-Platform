# Major Incident Review & Reporting Platform

A centralised ServiceNow platform for managing the end-to-end Major Incident Review lifecycle. The solution replaces fragmented, document-based activities with structured workflows for report preparation, collaborative review, approvals, SLA governance, PDF publication, controlled distribution, and audit-ready record management.

## Project Highlights

- Centralised Major Incident Report creation, review, approval, publication, and distribution within ServiceNow.
- Replaced Microsoft Word-based review activities with real-time, screen-based collaboration in ServiceNow.
- Integrated the Major Incident Review process with the existing Major Incident record and supporting incident data.
- Established structured lifecycle stages from Draft through Internal Review, Stakeholder Engagement, Stakeholder Review, Director Approval, Final Submission, and Closure.
- Introduced workflow-driven approvals, notifications, reminders, and SLA governance.
- Automated draft and final PDF generation, attachment management, and controlled report distribution.
- Improved traceability, auditability, data consistency, and compliance readiness across the post-incident review process.
- Created a scalable foundation for future AI-assisted drafting and insight generation.

## Business Value

The platform centralises the end-to-end Major Incident Review process within ServiceNow, replacing fragmented document-based activities with structured workflows, real-time review, controlled approvals, and automated report publication. This reduces administrative overhead, improves data consistency and process visibility, and enables Major Incident Managers and stakeholders to collaborate through a single governed record.

Centralised retention of MIR records, approvals, communications, and final reports also strengthens traceability, auditability, compliance readiness, and continuous service improvement after the active incident lifecycle.

## Problem Statement

The Major Incident Review process relied on fragmented workflows, manual data consolidation, Microsoft Word-based documentation, and separate review and distribution activities. This increased administrative effort, extended report turnaround times, introduced the risk of inconsistent or incomplete information, and limited visibility across the review lifecycle.

### Key Challenges

- Major Incident Reports were prepared through manual and fragmented document-based activities.
- Incident information required manual consolidation, increasing effort and the risk of data inconsistencies.
- Review coordination, approvals, notifications, and report distribution relied on separate activities outside a single governed workflow.
- Stakeholder review sessions depended on Microsoft Word documents rather than a shared, real-time system record.
- Limited end-to-end visibility made it difficult to track report status, ownership, approvals, and outstanding actions.
- Historical records and supporting evidence were not managed through a single audit-ready platform.

## Key Features

- Automatic creation of a dedicated MIR record following Major Incident resolution.
- Integration between Major Incident and MIR records to support consistent incident information.
- Structured lifecycle stages covering Draft, Internal Review, Stakeholder Engagement, Stakeholder Review, Director Approval, Final Submission, and Closure.
- Real-time, screen-based stakeholder review within ServiceNow, replacing reliance on Microsoft Word-based review documents.
- Workflow-driven internal review and director approval processes.
- SLA monitoring, reminder notifications, and escalation communications for time-sensitive review activities.
- State-based field controls that display, require, or protect information at the appropriate lifecycle stage.
- Automated generation and attachment of draft and final MIR PDF documents.
- Controlled final report distribution using standardised email templates.
- Centralised retention of MIR data, approvals, communications, attachments, and final reports for auditability and traceability.
